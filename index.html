who I am

A computer operator oversees and regulates the operation of computers, while hardware, software, and networks run efficiently. These are tasks, including monitoring performance, solving system errors, checking logs, and running routine backup systems. Their main job would involve collaboration with the IT team on implementing upgrades, resolving technical issues, and maintaining security. Some other general responsibilities may include data entry, generation of reports, and hardware maintenance.

I believe great interfaces are invisible — they get out of the user's way. My work is fast, accessible and built to last. When I'm not coding, you'll find me hiking or hunting for a good espresso.
